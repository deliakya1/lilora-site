# Lilora app artwork

Exported on 20 October 2026 from the owner-approved populated Build 12 artwork
in [Lilora's Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/bPJXvFWNCgxgyrSM4JVtam/Lilora?node-id=178-5921).
This set supersedes the earlier page `171:2`, which remains preserved in Figma.
The app repository's `docs/STATUS.md` and
`docs/store/release-assets/build-12-populated/manifest.json` identify this as the
current submitted artwork.

The 24 PNG files are complete Figma renders at their original canvas dimensions:
1320 × 2868 for iPhone and 2064 × 2752 for iPad, in English and Turkish. They show
real app screens with sample plant records, not customer accounts. Identification
and sharing compositions show the real welcome screens; they are not screenshots
of a live identification result or a connected household. No app build, generated
interface, new device mockup or production data was used. Existing cropping inside
the original compositions is preserved; no additional crop or device transformation
was applied.

Direct asset downloads were blocked by this execution environment. Figma's inline
PNG export was used at each node's original dimensions. The manifest records each
node, dimension, local file hash and responsive WebP derivative. PNG and WebP
compression differ from prior store deliveries; these are fresh exports of the
same approved frames, not claims of byte identity with the store upload.

Web derivatives in `../../images/home/app-*.webp` retain the complete composition
and aspect ratio. They are resized to 320, 480 and 720 pixels wide with Lanczos
resampling and WebP quality 87. Use the PNG as a fallback where needed; do not
load original print-size artwork for a small web preview.
