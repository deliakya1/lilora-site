# App Store launch assets

Supplied by the owner on 30 October 2026 from Apple's marketing toolbox. Apple's
artwork: never redraw, recolour or crop it. Public copies are served from
`https://lilora.app/assets/brand/app-store/…` and kept in this repository.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `badges/<lang>/black.svg`, `white.svg` | "Download on the App Store" badge |
| `badges/<lang>/pre-order-on-*.svg` | Pre-order badges (not used; the app is live) |
| `qr/lilora-app-store-qr-*.jpg` | Apple QR codes (400 × 400) for `https://apple.co/4yUrU3L`, which opens the U.S. listing of id6818604360. `white-icon` is used on the site. |
| `lilora-app-icon-1024.png` | App icon, 1024 × 1024 |

Badge languages: `en` (US-UK), `tr`, `es`, `de`, `fr`, `it`, `ja`, `ko`, `nl`,
`pt-BR`, `pt-PT` and `zh-Hans` (Apple file titles PTBR, PTPT and CNSC). The site
uses the black badge from `../app-store-badge/<site language>.svg`; `pt` is the
PTBR file. No Traditional Chinese file was supplied, so the owner chose on
30 October 2026 to use the Simplified Chinese badge on `zh-Hant` pages; swap
`../app-store-badge/zh-Hant.svg` when a Traditional Chinese badge is available.
