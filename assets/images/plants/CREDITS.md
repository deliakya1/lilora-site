# Plant library photo credits

Each plant profile on lilora.app uses one real photograph of the plant, taken by an iNaturalist observer and published under a Creative Commons licence that allows reuse. They are not generated images, not Lilora customer photos and not app screenshots. The same credit appears as a visible caption under each photo on every language version of the profile.

How they were chosen (22 October 2026):

- Candidate photos were found through the public GBIF occurrence API for iNaturalist observations of each species, filtered to CC BY 4.0 or CC0 licences. The original file was downloaded from the `inaturalist-open-data` bucket.
- Each photo was checked visually for the right plant, a clear view of the leaves and no people, text or brand marks. Licence and author come from the GBIF and iNaturalist records; re-check the observation page before reusing a photo elsewhere.
- Files were resized to 720 px on the long side and saved as WebP (quality 66) with Pillow. Metadata was stripped. Nothing else was edited unless stated.
- Where the photo shows a related species or cultivar, the caption names the photographed taxon (for example *Spathiphyllum wallisii* on the peace lily profile).
