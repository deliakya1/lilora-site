Lilora

A record you can come back to

Your plants, in one place

Keep plants, rooms, photos and notes together. Add a plant by hand, even if you do not know its species.

A check is not always a watering

Reminders ask you to check. Record watering and checks separately, so the next person can see what happened and when.

Start on your own, or share care

Start without an account; guest records stay on your device. An account lets you share a private household and use photo identification.

Manual care stays free

Manual plants, rooms, photos, notes and care history are free. Photo identification is optional, uses Pl@ntNet and offers suggestions for you to confirm.
