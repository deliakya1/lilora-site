Plants to consider

None of the 12 plants in our library is both listed non-toxic by the ASPCA and suited to this light. The plants below are on that list but want a different spot; read the light line before you buy.

    Before you choose

    • This is a starting point drawn from our 12 plant profiles, not a guarantee that a plant will thrive in your home. Temperature, watering and the exact spot matter too.
    • Toxicity information comes from the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center list for cats and dogs, as summarised on each profile. It covers only cats and dogs, the list is not a safety guarantee, and non-toxic does not mean a pet can eat the plant.
    • If a pet chews a plant or seems unwell, contact your vet.
    • The library has only 12 plants so far, so many good options are not here yet.
