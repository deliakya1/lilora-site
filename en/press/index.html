Lilora press resources

A few words about Lilora, and the original artwork to go with them.

Short description

Lilora is a shared houseplant notebook for iPhone and iPad. Keep plants, rooms, photos, notes and care history together, with separate records for checking and watering.

Long description

Lilora helps the people in a household keep a useful record of their plants. A plant can have a name, a room, a photo and notes. Reminders prompt a check; the person caring for the plant decides whether it needs water. Checking and watering have separate records, so someone else can see who did what and when.

Manual plants and care are free. You can begin without an account, with records stored on your device. An account adds private household sharing and optional photo identification through Pl@ntNet. Identification suggestions need your confirmation. Each account receives 3 free identifications, with an optional pack of 10 or Lilora Plus Monthly for 30 each month, without rollover.

The app is made for iPhone and iPad, with English and Turkish interfaces. Lilora is in App Store review and is not yet publicly available.
