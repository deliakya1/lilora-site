Lilora vs PictureThis: identification and everyday plant care

A short answer

PictureThis is worth examining when recognizing plants and exploring plant information are central to your needs. Lilora is designed around a household notebook, with optional identification alongside care records. PictureThis also documents collections and care reminders. Choose by what you want to do after learning a plant’s name.

What this comparison covers

As of 16 October 2026, Lilora is awaiting App Store review. PictureThis has a public U.S. App Store listing. This compares documented capabilities, not hands-on accuracy. It is published by Lilora, one of the products discussed. Features, prices and offers may change. PictureThis on the App Store

A name is the beginning of a plant’s story

You bring home a plant without a label. Your first question is its identity. Weeks later: When did you last check it? What changed after moving it? Did someone else already care for it?

An identification result helps you investigate a name. A notebook helps you remember observations and actions. Thinking about both moments makes it easier to decide what you need.

The documented features

Lilora describes optional Pl@ntNet-powered suggestions, reviewed before saving; photos, notes and history; check-first reminders; and a private household that shares plant records. Disease diagnosis is outside its described first release. Lilora overview · Lilora support

PictureThis describes photo identification, a collection and wishlist, watering and fertilizing reminders, problem-assessment tools and expert consultation. Check its current sharing options for your particular needs: a capability not established by our sources is not necessarily absent. PictureThis product page · FAQ

Identification: compare the process, not a headline number

This article does not rank accuracy. A fair performance comparison would need a defined set of plants, consistent photographs, verified reference identifications and a disclosed method. We have not conducted that test.

Ask practical questions: Can you review a result before accepting it? Correct a name? Keep records without running another identification? What happens when the image is unclear?

Lilora allows free manual species entry and asks you to review suggestions. PictureThis’s FAQ explains identification corrections and says identification needs a network connection. These details help you plan a workflow without assuming either product always gets a photo right. Lilora identification · PictureThis FAQ

Do not use an app result as permission to eat an unfamiliar plant or mushroom. PictureThis’s FAQ also cautions against tasting unfamiliar plants without professional help. PictureThis safety guidance

Everyday care: look beyond the camera

It would be misleading to call PictureThis only an identifier. Its FAQ documents watering and fertilizing reminders; its listing describes a collection. PictureThis FAQ · App Store description

Lilora puts photos, notes and care history in a notebook that can belong to a private household. Guest records stay on the device; signed-in household records are shared with members. Its check-first wording asks you to look before deciding what to record. Lilora storage and care

For your own evaluation, choose an ordinary week. Can you find a plant quickly? Can another carer understand the latest record? Can you tell a completed action from a reminder? These are suggested questions, not results from our testing.

Understand the paid offer before committing

The U.S. PictureThis listing includes multiple products named “PictureThis Pro,” including $39.99 entries. The label alone does not establish a billing period. Read the offer shown to your Apple account. PictureThis App Store listing

Lilora describes three initial identifications, a pack of 10 and a monthly Plus allowance of 30. Manual entry remains available without buying identifications. Regional prices are shown by Apple; we do not claim Lilora is less expensive. Lilora purchases

Check what the first charge covers, whether it renews, and what remains usable without a paid plan. A free download and a free trial are not the same promise.

A practical way to choose

  • For “What plant is this, and where can I learn more?”, investigate PictureThis’s identification and reference tools.
  • For “What happened to our plants, and who cared for them?”, explore Lilora’s household-notebook approach while launch is pending.
  • If both matter, compare the full process from photo to record to next care check. Do not choose entirely from a camera demonstration.
