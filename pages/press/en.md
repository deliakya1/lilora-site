    <section aria-labelledby="short-description"><h2 id="short-description">Short description</h2><p class="intro">Lilora is a shared houseplant notebook for iPhone and iPad. Keep plants, rooms, photos, notes and care history together, with separate records for checking and watering.</p></section>
    <section aria-labelledby="long-description"><h2 id="long-description">Long description</h2><p>Lilora helps the people in a household keep a useful record of their plants. A plant can have a name, a room, a photo and notes. Reminders prompt a check; the person caring for the plant decides whether it needs water. Checking and watering have separate records, so someone else can see who did what and when.</p><p>Manual plants and care are free. You can begin without an account, with records stored on your device. An account adds private household sharing and optional photo identification through Pl@ntNet. Identification suggestions need your confirmation. Each account receives 3 free identifications, with an optional pack of 10 or Lilora Plus Monthly for 30 each month, without rollover.</p><p>The app is made for iPhone and iPad, with English and Turkish interfaces. Lilora is in App Store review and is not yet publicly available.</p></section>
