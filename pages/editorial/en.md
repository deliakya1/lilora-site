<div class="wrap document-heading"><p class="eyebrow">LILORA</p><h1>Editorial approach</h1></div><div class="wrap document"><h2>Useful notes, with sources</h2><p>Lilora publishes these guides to help people observe their plants and coordinate care. We link to horticultural institutions and official product documentation. Articles distinguish published product claims from our own suggestions; they are not hands-on product tests unless explicitly described as such.</p><h2 id="about-our-content">About our content</h2><ul><li><strong>Research.</strong> Each guide starts from institutional sources: university extension services, the RHS, botanical gardens and, for pets, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center. Product facts come from the Lilora app itself and from official store listings.</li><li><strong>Drafting with AI help.</strong> Pages are drafted with the help of AI writing tools and then edited by Lilora. AI is a drafting aid, not a source: no fact is published because a tool suggested it.</li><li><strong>Fact-checking.</strong> Every plant-care claim is checked against the sources linked inline on the page, and every app claim against the current app text. Translations are reviewed as separate pages, not machine output left as is.</li><li><strong>Dates.</strong> Each article shows when it was published and when it was last reviewed. The review date changes only after a real check or revision.</li><li><strong>Images.</strong> Illustrative scenes may be generated and are labelled as such; they never show a real user’s plant or a diagnosis. App screenshots are real captures of the Lilora app with sample records.</li></ul><h2>A clear commercial relationship</h2><p>This is the Lilora website, so we have an interest in our own app. Comparisons identify that relationship, describe alternatives fairly, and avoid invented ratings or absent-feature claims. There are no affiliate links in this collection. Store features, prices and availability can change by country and version.</p><h2>Care and uncertainty</h2><p>A due check is not an instruction to water. Plant identification suggestions need confirmation. General articles cannot diagnose a particular plant or establish that it is safe for a person or pet. We do not claim horticultural qualifications or guaranteed outcomes.</p><h2>Corrections and updates</h2><p>Each article shows its review date and sources. Dates change when content is substantively checked or revised. Send a correction with the page URL and supporting source to <a href="mailto:support@lilora.app">support@lilora.app</a>.</p><h2 id="imagery">Images and illustrations</h2><p>Generated images, when used, provide atmosphere. They are not app screenshots, photos of a user’s plant, or evidence for identifying or diagnosing a plant. App artwork shows real Lilora screens. Image credits record the source and any applicable licence.</p><aside class="app-note"><p class="eyebrow">lilora</p><h2>A notebook for shared care</h2><p>A reminder is a reason to check your plant, not an instruction to water it.</p><p class="availability">Lilora for iPhone and iPad is available on the App Store.</p><a class="button" data-analytics-cta="page-body" href="/support/">Meet Lilora <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></aside></div>
