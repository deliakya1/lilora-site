<section class="document-heading wrap"><p class="eyebrow">LILORA / SUPPORT</p><h1>A little help for your garden.</h1><p class="intro">Answers to common questions, and a way to reach us.</p></section><article class="document wrap"><h2 id="contact">Contact</h2>
<p>Write to <a href="mailto:support@lilora.app">support@lilora.app</a>. Tell us which iPhone or iPad and iOS version you use and what happened; screenshots help. We answer in English or Turkish.</p>
<p><a class="button outline" href="/en/contact/#contact-form">Open the contact form</a></p>
<h2 id="faq">Common questions</h2><div class="faq-list"><details class="faq-item" id="language"><summary><h3>How do I change the language?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Open Settings → Language and choose “English” or “Türkçe”. The choice is saved on this device. “Use phone language” returns to automatic selection: Turkish when your primary phone language is Turkish, otherwise English.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="storage"><summary><h3>Where are my plants stored?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Without an account, on this device only. Deleting the app deletes them.</p>
<p>If you sign in and create or join a household, its plants, care history and photos are stored online and shared with its members. Signing out removes that household's local cache from this device; it does not delete the household.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="delete-account"><summary><h3>How do I delete my account?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Use Settings → Delete account. The app explains what will happen to your household before you confirm. Type DELETE to confirm. Other members keep the shared records with former-member attribution; if you are its only member, the household is closed and its data is removed. Deleting the app alone does not delete an online account. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your App Store account settings.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="care-outcomes"><summary><h3>Why does Lilora say "check" instead of "water"?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>A due check means looking at the plant and the soil. Water only when the soil needs it, then record "Watered"; if it does not, record "Checked, no water needed".</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="reminders"><summary><h3>I don't get reminders.</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Turn reminders on in the app (Today or Settings), then make sure notifications for Lilora are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications. Reminders arrive at 9:00 local time on the day a check or watering reminder is due.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="correct-care"><summary><h3>Can I correct a care entry?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Open the plant, tap the entry in History, then Correct this entry or Remove entry. The earlier value stays visible in the history.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="remove-plant"><summary><h3>How do I remove a plant?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Open it and choose Archive. Archived plants keep their history and can be restored from My plants → Archived plants. “Delete plant” permanently removes the plant, photo and all care history, including unsent entries. In a shared household it removes the plant for everyone. This cannot be undone.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="photo-identification"><summary><h3>How does photo identification work?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Sign in, open Add plant or a plant without a species, and tap Add a plant with AI. Lilora shows a few suggestions from Pl@ntNet; nothing is saved until you choose one. You can always type the species yourself for free. Your first 3 identifications are free. An identification is used only when it returns suggestions; no match, errors and timeouts are free, and trying the same photo again is never charged twice.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="more-identifications"><summary><h3>How do I get more identifications?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Settings → Usage &amp; purchases (screen title “Identifications”) offers a pack of 10 identifications and the “Lilora Plus Monthly” subscription with 30 identifications each month. Prices are shown by the App Store in your currency. Monthly identifications end with that month and don't carry over.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="restore-purchases"><summary><h3>I bought identifications on another device or reinstalled the app.</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Sign in to the same Lilora account, then tap Settings → Usage &amp; purchases → Restore purchases. Purchases belong to the Lilora account that made them.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="cancel-subscription"><summary><h3>How do I cancel Lilora Plus or ask for a refund?</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>Tap Manage subscription in Settings → Usage &amp; purchases, or open your App Store account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>.</p></div></details><details class="faq-item" id="privacy-requests"><summary><h3>Privacy requests</h3></summary><div class="faq-answer"><p>For access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights, see <a href="/en/privacy/#rights">Your privacy rights</a>. The policy explains the KVKK and GDPR request channels and deadlines.</p></div></details></div></article>
