<section class="document-heading wrap"><p class="eyebrow">LILORA</p><h1>About Lilora</h1><p class="intro">A plant notebook for the things you notice and the care you give.</p></section><article class="document wrap prose"><h2>Lilora at a glance</h2><p>Lilora is a houseplant notebook app for iPhone and iPad. It keeps plants, rooms, photos, notes and care history together, records checking and watering separately, and lets a private household share that record. It is for people who want a clear care history, including households where several people look after the same plants.</p><ul><li><strong>Free:</strong> adding plants by hand, rooms, photos, notes, care history and reminders. (<a href="/en/download/#free-care">Plans</a>)</li><li><strong>Optional photo identification:</strong> suggestions from <a href="https://plantnet.org/">Pl@ntNet</a> that you confirm before saving; 3 free per account, then a 10-identification pack or Lilora Plus Monthly with 30 per month. Needs an account. (<a href="/en/support/#photo-identification">Support</a>)</li><li><strong>Reminders</strong> prompt a check at 9:00 local time; they are not an instruction to water. (<a href="/en/support/#reminders">Support</a>)</li><li><strong>Accounts:</strong> optional. Guest records stay on the device; sharing, identification and purchases need an account. (<a href="/en/support/#storage">Support</a>)</li><li><strong>App languages:</strong> English and Turkish. The website is in six languages. (<a href="/en/support/#language">Support</a>)</li><li><strong>Availability:</strong> awaiting App Store review; no public download yet. (<a href="/en/download/">Download status</a>)</li><li><strong>Contact:</strong> <a href="mailto:support@lilora.app">support@lilora.app</a>, answered in English or Turkish.</li></ul><h2>What Lilora does not do</h2><p>No Android or web app, no disease diagnosis or symptom analysis, no light meter or sensors, and no guarantee that an identification is correct. Guest records are not backed up and are deleted with the app.</p><h2>Notice before you water</h2><p>A reminder is a reason to check the plant and its soil. Water when it needs water, then record what you did. Checking without watering is care, too.</p><h2>Keep the story together</h2><p>Keep plant photos, notes and care history in one place. Start without an account and keep your guest garden on your device.</p><h2>Share care at home</h2><p>Create or join a private household to share plants, photos and care records with the people you live with.</p><h2>Independent publishing</h2><p>Lilora is independently published by Umut OREN in Türkiye.
